# Student Training & Placement Impact Analytics

A Power BI dashboard analysing how training programs affect student placement outcomes.

## Problem Statement
Do aptitude, coding and mock interview training improve placement chances?
Which students are at risk of not getting placed?

## Dataset
500 student records (synthetic dataset created for demonstration) with department, CGPA, arrears, internship, training details, placement status, company and package.

## Tools Used
Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Excel

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Overview:** KPIs, placed vs not placed, department-wise placement %
2. **Training Impact:** training status, mock interviews, soft skills, risk level
3. **Academic & Company Insights:** CGPA band, arrears, top recruiters, average package

## Key Insights
- Overall placement rate: 62.2%
- Fully trained students: 75.3% placed vs 36.5% with no training (38.8 point gap)
- Placement rises from 46.2% (0 mock interviews) to 85.7% (5 mock interviews)
- High-risk students: 39.0% placed vs 81.5% for low-risk students
- Placement drops sharply with arrears: 70.5% (0 arrears) to 16.7% (3 arrears)

## Recommendations
- Make aptitude and coding training mandatory
- Increase mock interview sessions to at least 3 per student
- Provide early support to students flagged as high risk
